01 / RESEARCH TOOLS
LIVE / PUBLIC BETA

ResearchLens AI

ResearchLens AI is my ongoing web app for exploring research papers. Upload a PDF, get a simpler explanation in English or Bengali, and ask questions about what you’re reading.

The app shows source passages alongside its answers so you can check the original paper. You can also compare papers, export citations, and choose to save your workspace in your browser.

What I’m exploring

The questions behind my work.

I’m interested in finding useful information—and understanding whether we can trust the explanations AI gives us.

01

Improving Search Accuracy in a Healthcare Research Paper Database

For my ESP project, I’m extending ResearchLens AI with a searchable collection of open-access healthcare papers. I plan to compare keyword search with semantic search using the same papers and questions, looking at relevance and response time.

How do keyword and semantic search compare when finding relevant healthcare research?

02

Evaluating the Reliability of Explainable AI Methods for Medical Text Classification

In a separate independent study, I’m planning to use public medical text data to explore how reliably explanation methods describe a classifier’s behavior. An explanation can sound convincing; I want to understand how well it reflects what the model actually does.

When can we rely on an explanation of a medical text classifier?
